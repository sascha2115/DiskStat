Files being cleaned from a removable volume:

Anywhere on the volume:
.DS_Store     Finder's icon positions and window sizes
._*           AppleDouble sidecars (extended attributes, resource forks).
              These are what Kodi and Windows see as duplicate media files.
@eaDir        folders holding metadata for over-long filenames (FAT/exFAT only)

At the top level of the volume:
.Spotlight-V100   Spotlight's search index for the volume
.fseventsd        file system change journal
.TemporaryItems   macOS scratch space

Never cleaned:
.Trashes     the user's deleted files -- that is data, not clutter

The startup volume is never cleaned. Everything removed here is regenerated by
macOS, so a clean only survives until the Mac next browses the drive -- which is
why cleaning and ejecting are done together as the final step.
